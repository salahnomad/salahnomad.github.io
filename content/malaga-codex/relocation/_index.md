The **Mediterranean Codex** exists because I made those mistakes for you. Every guide in this series is a field manual: real 2026 rents per neighbourhood, acoustic risk scores, verified Black Book contacts (zero commissions), and step‑by‑step bureaucracy blueprints. No fluff. No guesswork.

These are the guides I wish someone had handed me when I arrived in Málaga with a suitcase and a broken lease.
